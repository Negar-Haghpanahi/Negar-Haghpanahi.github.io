Research

Clinical digital twin for severe TBI prognosis

TBI Model Systems National Database · Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch

A digital twin that predicts recovery milestones after moderate-to-severe traumatic brain injury and updates its forecast as recovery unfolds, with reinforcement learning for long-term follow-up outcomes.

AdaExit: adaptive early-exit inference for edge devices

2025 · PyTorch

A confidence-based early-exit framework for on-device inference. Cuts energy use by 50–60% with less than 1% accuracy loss across six wearable-sensor benchmarks.

EMI: energy-aware early exit for wearable IoT

2026 · PyTorch, TensorFlow

Bayesian optimization finds the best energy–accuracy trade-offs, and a lookup-table controller picks the exit at runtime. Deployed on NVIDIA GPU and Raspberry Pi hardware.
